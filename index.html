🌟 Profil Profesional

Dosen dan akademisi profesional di bidang Keperawatan dan Kesehatan Masyarakat dengan jabatan fungsional Lektor 200 di Universitas Duta Bangsa Surakarta. Memiliki latar belakang pendidikan multidisiplin yang kokoh mencakup Sarjana Keperawatan (S.Kep), Sarjana Kesehatan Masyarakat (SKM - Universitas Muhammadiyah Semarang), serta Magister Kedokteran Keluarga (M.Kes - Universitas Sebelas Maret Surakarta).

Berpengalaman lebih dari dua dekade dalam pelayanan keperawatan klinis rumah sakit, riset survei epidemiologi nasional Kemenkes RI (Riskesdas & Rifaskes), penguji OSCE regional Jawa Tengah, serta pembicara tamu internasional di Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL) Malaysia.

Fokus riset terapan meliputi manajemen asuhan keperawatan degeneratif (CKD on HD, Stroke, Diabetes Melitus), promosi kesehatan tidur lansia dan anak, serta integrasi teknologi kecerdasan buatan (Chatbot AI dan Expert System) dalam pencegahan stunting dan kesehatan ibu.

Distinguished Assistant Professor (Lektor 200) and Senior Lecturer in Nursing and Public Health at Universitas Duta Bangsa Surakarta with more than two decades of dedicated expertise in clinical nursing, health sciences education, and healthcare research. Holds multidisciplinary degrees comprising a Bachelor of Nursing (S.Kep), a Bachelor of Public Health (SKM) from Universitas Muhammadiyah Semarang, and a Master of Health Sciences in Family Medicine (M.Kes) from Universitas Sebelas Maret (UNS).

Extensively experienced across clinical hospital care (dr. Oen Hospital), nationwide health surveillance enumeration (Ministry of Health RI - Riskesdas & Rifaskes), healthcare vocational institution leadership, national clinical OSCE examination, and international keynote lecturing at Universiti Kuala Lumpur (UniKL) Malaysia.

Applied research interests include degenerative disease care (CKD on hemodialysis, Stroke, Diabetes Mellitus), ergonomic exercise, sleep hygiene, and artificial intelligence integration (AI Chatbots and Expert Systems) in childhood stunting prevention and maternal health.
